About Me

My name is Oyiza Salawu, a recent graduate from the University of Lagos with a background in Cell Biology and Genetics and a strong passion for data analysis and insights. While my academic training is rooted in the life sciences, it laid the foundation for my transition into data science.

Both biology and data analysis share a common core — asking the right questions, identifying patterns, and drawing meaningful conclusions from complex data. Whether working with genetic information or business datasets, I am driven by the same goal: to uncover insights and tell clear, impactful stories.

I believe that data tells stories, and I am dedicated to transforming complex, unstructured data into actionable insights that support informed decision-making. My analytical mindset, combined with technical skills in tools like Python, Excel, and data visualization, allows me to approach problems systematically and deliver meaningful solutions.

Currently, I am continuously learning and building a portfolio of real-world projects, further strengthening my skills and deepening my understanding of data-driven problem solving. I am passionate about using data to drive growth, improve decision-making, and create value.
