Metropolitan Medical Centre

Patient Wait Time Analysis & Optimization

Analysis done to strategically analyze patient admission data to improve clinical outcomes and reduce healthcare costs. The initiative supports data-driven decision making to enhance operational efficiency and strengthen overall hospital performance.

Objective: Reduce patient wait times and improve ED efficiency

Data Size: 10,000+ patient records

Tools Used: SQL, Python (Pandas, Matplotlib), Excel

Timeline: 8 weeks

Key Insights:
  • check_circle Peak arrival times occur between 8-10 AM with 40% of daily volume
  • check_circle Triage process is the primary bottleneck, averaging 25 minutes per patient
  • check_circle Advanced cases require 60% longer wait times than routine cases
  • check_circle Staffing optimization can reduce wait times by 15-20%
